# Built-in image-generation prompts

## Rabbit mark

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished standalone rabbit logo symbol for Chinki, a friendly digital gift-card app. A clearly recognizable rabbit with two long upright ears, rounded head, simple small eye and compact soft body or cheek silhouette. Elegant bold flat black silhouette, with one subtle white negative-space detail. Friendly and memorable, not aggressive, not a generic abstract letter, not a cat. Strong legibility as a small app icon. Center the single mark with generous transparent padding. No text, no mockup, no gradients, no shadows, no border, no watermark. Deliver a clean high-resolution transparent PNG.
